# Thornwake Hall of Fame

## Alden Wayfarer — the first explorer
Named by Tom and Noor (2026-10-05). The little blocky fellow in the green tunic, brown trousers and boots who has walked
every world of the rewrite since the first build: across the first rivers, up the first mountains, into the first
biomes, under the first stars. In game he is the player character of the World Lab.

## Art direction note (Tom's reference image, round 7)
An AI-generated inspiration picture of Alden on a mountain top — not a fidelity target, a mood target:
* Alden with a bedroll backpack and a walking staff, looking out over the land.
* Big, chunky, blocky boulders with flat faces at his feet, wild flowers (white, orange, red) and grass between them.
* Low clouds and mist lying in the valleys, snowy jagged peaks behind, golden low sun with warm light and long shadows.
* A land of lakes, islands, rivers, waterfalls off cliffs, autumn-coloured trees among conifers, castles and ruins on
  crags and islands (later: points of interest from the World Site Editor).
